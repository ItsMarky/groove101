Christmas Party Band Hire in Kent & London: A Planning Guide

By Groove 101 · Published 18 October 2026

Now taking Christmas bookings: Groove 101 brings live soul, funk and party favourites to work celebrations and private parties across Kent, London and the South East. If you are organising this year's Christmas event, start with your date and venue, then build a music plan around your guests and schedule.

Check your Christmas party date first

Send your preferred date, venue or town and approximate timings when you enquire. If your date is flexible, include alternatives. Availability needs to be checked for each event; contact Groove 101 for a confirmed answer and a tailored quote.

Choose a band that suits the room

A staff dinner, a client celebration and a private Christmas party need different pacing. Decide whether you want conversation over dinner followed by dancing, or an evening centred on the dance floor. Watch the showreel to hear Groove 101's soul, funk and party sound before deciding.

Tell the band about the audience and any musical preferences. If Christmas songs are important to your event, discuss specific requests in advance so the repertoire can be confirmed.

Compare Christmas party band costs fairly

Use the current band packages as a starting point and request a quote for your date, location and schedule. Check the agreed line-up, live performance time, PA, lighting, playlist music and any travel or additional charges. Ask what happens if you need an earlier setup or a later finish.

For a company booking, confirm who approves the quote, who signs the agreement and what your finance team needs to arrange payment. Keep the venue coordinator and the band's contact details together so practical questions reach the right person.

A practical Christmas party running order

  1. Setup: arrange band access before guests arrive where possible.
  2. Arrival and dinner: choose music at a level that allows conversation.
  3. Speeches or awards: allow a clear slot before dancing starts.
  4. First live set: bring the energy up after the meal.
  5. Break: keep music playing while guests visit the bar or catch up.
  6. Final live set: finish the party within the venue's music cutoff.

Agree the actual set lengths and timings in your quote. Avoid scheduling the main live set at the same time as food service or lengthy presentations.

What to ask a Kent or London venue

  • Is live amplified music allowed, and is there a sound limiter?
  • When must live music and background music finish?
  • Where will the band perform, and is there room for dancing?
  • How do musicians unload, park and access the room?
  • Are suitable power sockets available near the performance area?
  • Does the venue need insurance documents or other information in advance?

Groove 101 usually needs around 90 minutes to set up, an approximately 5 × 3 metre performance area and at least two standard 13A sockets. Confirm the details for your booking before committing to a room layout.

Keep the booking details in one place

Your final plan should include the venue address, event contact, access time, soundcheck arrangements, live set timings, break music and finish time. Share any changes with the band and venue together, especially if dinner, speeches or transport alter the schedule.

Christmas booking questions

Can we book for a private Christmas party?

Yes. Enquire with your date and venue for a private celebration, as well as work and corporate parties.

Can you provide music between live sets?

Groove 101 offers playlist music between live sets. Confirm the timing and coverage included in your chosen package.

Do we need to have the whole evening planned before enquiring?

No. A date, location and rough outline are enough to begin checking availability and discussing a suitable package.
