A little planning goes a long way

A good fit for your venue.

Share your venue’s live music requirements before booking so we can discuss the practical details together.

Explore our venue guides, including Tunbridge Wells and Tonbridge and South East London. Always confirm current requirements with your venue.

Useful details to check

  • Setup: usually around 90 minutes.
  • Space: approximately 5 × 3 metres for the band.
  • Power: at least two standard 13A sockets.
  • Sound: share limiter settings and music finish times.
  • Access: confirm unloading, parking and room access.

Exact requirements depend on your chosen line-up and venue.
