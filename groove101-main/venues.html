Before you book

Five questions for your venue.

The room and its music policy matter as much as the setting. Confirm these details early so your band can assess suitability.

Read the live music venue checklist →
  1. Sound limits

    Is there a sound limiter? Ask for its settings and the venue’s policy for live bands.

  2. Performance space

    Where would the band set up, and how much space remains for dancing?

  3. Power

    What suitable power sockets are available near the performance area?

  4. Access and setup

    Confirm unloading, parking and when setup and soundcheck are allowed.

  5. Finish times

    Check separate cutoff times for live music and recorded music.
